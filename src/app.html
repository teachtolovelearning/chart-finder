How we match charts to your state

Every chart is aligned to Common Core (ELA and math), NGSS (science), Texas TEKS and Florida B.E.S.T. When you choose your state, each chart shows the alignment that fits your state best, with a label for how close it is.

  • Exact matchYour state uses these standards, sometimes under its own name, so the codes are the ones in your curriculum.
  • Close matchYour state revised Common Core or NGSS. Most standards carry over, though wording or numbering can differ on a few.
  • Matched by our teamYour state wrote or revised its own standards, so we read your state's standards for each grade and matched every chart to the standard a teacher would cite. Check the wording in your state's document if it matters for your lesson plans.
  • Closest referenceYour state wrote its own standards. The skills overlap heavily, so we show Common Core or NGSS; look up the matching code in your state's document for lesson plans.
